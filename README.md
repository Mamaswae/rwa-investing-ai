Overview

RWA Investing AI is an autonomous investment agent designed to optimize capital allocation across tokenized Real-World Assets (RWA) and DeFi yield opportunities. The agent continuously analyzes market conditions, risk metrics, and on-chain data to identify sustainable yield strategies and improve portfolio performance.

Key Features
-Autonomous Portfolio Management
-Monitors RWA and DeFi markets in real time.
-Allocates capital based on predefined risk parameters.
-Rebalances positions automatically.

Yield Optimization
-Identifies high-quality yield opportunities.
-Compares returns across multiple protocols.
-Maximizes risk-adjusted performance.

Risk Monitoring
-Tracks protocol health and liquidity conditions.
-Detects abnormal market behavior.
-Reduces exposure during elevated risk events.

On-Chain Execution
-Executes transactions through verified smart contracts.
-Maintains transparent and auditable operations.
-Supports multi-protocol integrations.
